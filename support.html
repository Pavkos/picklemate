Pickle Mate

Support

Help for Pickleball Watch Scorer on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Common Questions

How do I start a match?

Open the app, choose New Match, set the match type, then use the scoring buttons during play. The app keeps the serving side and score state for you.

How does voice scoring work?

Tap the microphone button during a match and say a scoring command such as "we scored", "they scored", "side out", or "undo". Microphone and speech recognition permissions are required.

Why is my Apple Watch not syncing?

Make sure the iPhone and Apple Watch are paired, Bluetooth is enabled, and both apps have been opened recently. If sync still looks stale, reopen the match on iPhone and then open the Watch app again.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple and must use the same Apple ID that purchased the lifetime unlock.

Is my match data backed up online?

No. The app is local-first and does not run a separate account system or server backup.
